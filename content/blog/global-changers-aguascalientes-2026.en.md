On October 23 and 24, 2026, Global Changers was held at the Aguascalientes Convention Center. The poster presents it as an Expo & Summit and also as a Wellness Fest & Summit. Brenda Guadalupe Caballero Lara took part as a speaker with «La IA no tiene conciencia, tú sí» (AI has no consciousness, you do).

As she announced it before the event, the talk started from an everyday scene: tools that automate decisions, generate content and answer in seconds. In that landscape, what no artificial intelligence does for anyone is judgment. Three questions followed. What can be handed over, and what should not be given up. What the real risks of that advance are. How to live with these tools without losing what makes us human.

In the idea she published with the invitation, artificial intelligence can offer ideas, sort data and show trends. The creative spark and the human touch, she wrote, come only from the person. That is why the conversation is no longer whether we will use AI, but how to use it without ceasing to be human. After the gathering she wrote that the space was a place to share, connect and reflect on the present and future of artificial intelligence, and to put its ethical, conscious use on the table: a more human, responsible technology, within reach of the person using it.

The gathering crosses wellbeing, technology and purpose. [Líder Empresarial](https://www.liderempresarial.com/7-eventos-imperdibles-en-aguascalientes-durante-octubre-de-2026/) described it as a meeting on wellbeing, healthy habits and personal development, and included it in the state's October calendar.

Brenda Guadalupe Caballero Lara is director of AI ethics and responsible use at the [Startup and Innovation Cluster](/en/quienes-somos/).
