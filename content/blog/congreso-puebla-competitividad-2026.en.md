## The CSI at the advisory councils

On September 14, 2026, **Rodrigo Yamil Álvarez Moisés**, president of the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/), represented the association at the session of the Business and Academic Advisory Councils of the [Congress of the State of Puebla](https://congresopuebla.gob.mx/).

Deputies **Ana Laura Gómez Ramírez**, chair of the Business Advisory Council, and **María Soledad Amieva Zamora**, chair of the Academic Advisory Council, led the session. Those councils open a dialogue among the Congress, business, and academia so different voices are heard before legislation moves forward.

## Competitiveness, technology, and artificial intelligence

The table moved two pieces that sit at the center of the startup ecosystem.

The first is digital wellbeing. In a first stage, the proposal covers rules for device use in schools and the responsible use of information technologies and artificial intelligence.

The second is a new Competitiveness Law. Deputy Ana Laura Gómez Ramírez shared the proposal she is preparing. Among its pieces are a State Competitiveness Council and a Competitiveness Observatory.

The group also discussed organizing the next Matilde Montoya Chair.

For the Cluster, that session is where innovation leaves the speech and enters the legislative agenda: talent, technology, and a responsible use of artificial intelligence as part of Puebla's competitiveness.

We thank the State Congress and deputies Ana Laura Gómez Ramírez and María Soledad Amieva Zamora for the space to bring the voice of the startup and innovation ecosystem.
