On Saturday, November 14, 2026, Puebla joined the national start of [Hackatón México](https://hackatonmexico.com/). The in-person kickoff was at Workósfera Coworking, in La Paz, from 11:00 to 14:00. [Luma](https://luma.com/jro3jq5m) recorded 69 people there.

That Saturday the Cursor Hackatón México opened the same day in seven cities: Mexico City, Monterrey, Guadalajara, Puebla, Villahermosa, León, and Culiacán. The brief, as the hackathon site publishes it, is four weeks to build payment experiences with CoDi and the [pey](https://mipey.mx/) API: a charge, an action, and a result someone can see. CoDi charges and pays instantly between accounts, with no fee per transaction. The same site places December 14 as the day new national rules for those payments take effect.

People can take part alone or on a team, and online from anywhere in the country. Submissions close on December 12. Featured projects are announced on the 14th. The organizers' stated goal is to make this the largest hackathon in Mexico's history. On prizes, the site speaks of more than a million pesos in value across prizes, credits, and resources, and says the breakdown will be announced once it is confirmed.

[Kevin Jiménez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevinjimenezosorno), a SpaceXAI ambassador and host of the gathering, later wrote that other technology events were on in the city that day and that turnout still beat what they expected. People came from Puebla, and also from Tlaxcala and Veracruz. There were students and working professionals, and teams formed through the morning.

SpaceXAI's artificial-intelligence credits were reserved for people who attended the kickoff. Anyone who could not be there can still build online through December 12. Registration remains at [hackatonmexico.com](https://hackatonmexico.com/).

The gathering is organized by [SpaceXAI Ambassadors](https://spacexaiambassadors.notion.site/SpaceXAI-Ambassadors-1c3ba798b53c81f1b11dc68db87d3ad8), [AI Builders México](https://aibuilders.mx/), and pey. The [Startup and Innovation Cluster](/en/quienes-somos/) joined from Puebla. [Álvaro Castillo](/en/fundadores/alvaro-castillo), cluster manager, co-hosted with Jiménez.
