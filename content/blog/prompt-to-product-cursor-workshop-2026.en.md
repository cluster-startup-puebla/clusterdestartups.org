## Build, don't just listen

On Thursday, August 27, 2026, the Cluster of Startups and Innovation A.C. and **Cursor Puebla** organized **Prompt to Product**, a beginner-level buildathon at Workósfera Coworking (Cholula 35, La Paz, Puebla). It was an **official Cursor event**, with community hosts Kevin Jiménez and Vahagn Chavushyan.

Registration **closed with a waiting list** and 46 sign-ups, in a capacity designed for seated work with laptops: 50 seats with tables, power outlets and stable internet for every device. The idea was simple and demanding at once: every participant walked away with **a working tool** they built themselves during the session.

## How the session went

The workshop introduced Cursor and AI agents with a live demonstration, then quickly moved into building:

- **Guided building**: everyone built the same starter project to learn the essential flow — write a prompt, iterate, see the result live.
- **Your own project**: each participant scoped their own idea and built it with hands-on support from the team, with 20 USD in Cursor credits included per participant.
- **Demos and community**: closing with each participant presenting their project and a live raffle.

The profile was deliberately beginner: no prior programming experience required — just an idea and the willingness to build it.

## A line, three events

Prompt to Product continues the AI community line the Cluster drives in Puebla, following v0 Prompt to Production in February and the Cafe Cursor in May, always with the same mechanism: tool credits, mandatory laptop and live building.

To hear about the next sessions, follow us or reach out: community line workshops are announced first among [Cluster](/membresias) membership.
