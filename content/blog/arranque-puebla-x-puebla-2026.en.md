On Wednesday, November 18, 2026, the Hotel Quinta Real hosted Puebla x Puebla, the session with which the [Puebla Technology Observatory](https://observatorio.clusterdestartups.org/blog/arranque-puebla-x-puebla-2026) opened a standing program of listening to industry. Between registration, at 8:30, and the close, at 11:35, 114 people gathered.

The format was a working breakfast. The morning was for hearing the context and for each company to write down the technological need of its operation. The aim is to identify those needs and connect them with founders and startups that can solve them.

From 9:50 to 10:30 the companies built the observatory: each answered from its own sector. The result will be an aggregated map on which no company name appears. The gathering brought owners, general managers, and operations directors from 13 sectors: automotive, agronomy, food, plastics, metalworking, tourism, logistics, textile, gastronomy, hospital care, construction, furniture, and graphic arts.

At 10:30 a panel brought Puebla and Nuevo León together. Silvia Ávila Cholula, president of the Puebla Plastics and Circular Economy Cluster, and Arturo Rech, president and CEO of MexHub de Economía Circular, spoke about the circular economy as a plant-floor decision. Fausto López Aguilar, international treaties manager at Volkswagen de México and vice president of COMCE Sur, then spoke about the advantages of adopting technology in Puebla industry ahead of the USMCA.

At the close, [Yamil Álvarez](/en/fundadores/yamil-alvarez), president of the [Startup and Innovation Cluster](/en/quienes-somos/), explained the open-innovation model: instead of each company developing its solutions alone, industry connects with founders and startups already working on them. He closed with an invitation to collaborate. «If you want to do it, that is what the Cluster is for», he said.

The breakfast is the launch. The observatory will continue with regular sessions and with meetings by sector. A company can also answer the diagnosis on its own, without waiting for the next session. The anonymous sector map will be released a few weeks later.

The full account is on the Observatory: [Arranca Puebla x Puebla](https://observatorio.clusterdestartups.org/blog/arranque-puebla-x-puebla-2026).
