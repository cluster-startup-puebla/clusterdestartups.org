## Visit to the Aeronautical University in Querétaro

On Friday, September 25, 2026, the [Aeronautical University in Querétaro (UNAQ)](https://www.unaq.edu.mx/) received the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/) of the state of Puebla. The university recorded the visit this way: the Cluster was represented by its president, **Rodrigo Yamil Álvarez Moisés**, and its vice president, **Álvaro Castillo Carreño**.

During the meeting, UNAQ presented its academic, scientific, and technological capabilities. The purpose was to open collaboration for **Space Tech** startups and for new business models in niche technologies.

## What UNAQ already builds

UNAQ sits in the Querétaro Aerospace Park, in Colón. Its [entrepreneurship](https://www.unaq.edu.mx/servicios/emprendimiento/) area incubates high-impact projects, preferably in aerospace. Its [technology development](https://www.unaq.edu.mx/servicios/desarrollo-tecnologico/) area does engineering with the aeronautical and space industry; among its public partners is MXSPACE, with which it launched the university's first Querétaro nanosatellite to the stratosphere. Student teams such as [Space Dragons](https://www.unaq.edu.mx/soyunaq/equipos-representativos/space-dragons/) and [KA'AN SAT](https://www.unaq.edu.mx/soyunaq/equipos-representativos/kaan-sat/) work on rocketry and pico-satellites.

That is the ground on which the visit makes sense. Space Tech and niche technologies do not come out of a presentation: they come out of labs, workshops, and people who already design hardware, materials, and systems. UNAQ has that knowledge. Puebla has [startups](/en/empresas/) that need to turn it into a company.

## From the visit to the work

The day before, the CSI had opened a [dialogue with Querétaro's technology ecosystem](/en/blog/dialogo-puebla-queretaro-ecosistema-2026/). UNAQ is the next step of that conversation: taking the dialogue into a university that trains aerospace and space talent.

For Puebla, the collaboration works if UNAQ's talent, innovation, and knowledge meet technology-based projects from the Cluster. A Puebla Space Tech startup can gain a lab, mentorship, and an industrial sector that already exists in Querétaro. The university gains teams that carry that knowledge into a business model. The September 25 visit opens that possibility.
