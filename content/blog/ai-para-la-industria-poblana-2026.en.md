## From the production floor to the data

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C. organized on Friday, May 8, 2026 the breakfast workshop **«Del piso de producción al dato»**, aimed at industry from the municipality of Amozoc de Mota and Puebla — owners, plant managers and line leads — at the Palacio Julio Hotel in Puebla's historic center.

The event, held with the institutional collaboration of **SECIHTI and CANACINTRA**, launched the **AI para la Industria Poblana** program, the Cluster's work line that brings diagnosis and technology adoption to the regional production floor. The opening featured remarks by the head of SECIHTI.

## What was worked on during the session

The session, free of charge for participants, was built around the language of operations, not design:

- **Two real success cases** of digitalized industry in Amozoc and Puebla, in the automotive and plastics sectors, presented by plants that have already modernized their operation.
- **Live Industry 4.0 self-diagnostic**: an anonymous form answered from the phone that returned a diagnosis and suggested actions to each participant immediately, with aggregated results projected in the room to read the state of the local industry.
- **OEE conceptual framework** (overall equipment effectiveness) presented by the Cluster as the entry point to the digitalization conversation.
- **Exhibition area** with two Cluster companies: **Kotemah**, with exoskeletons for production operators, and **Oxtron**, turning emissions into a competitive advantage.

## A program, not a one-off event

«Del piso de producción al dato» was the launch of the AI para la Industria Poblana program, conceived as a continuous line of diagnosis, cases and technology adoption for the manufacturing industry of the municipality of Amozoc and the Puebla metropolitan area.

Companies and industry professionals interested in the program's next sessions can reach out at [prensa@clusterdestartups.org](mailto:prensa@clusterdestartups.org) or apply for Cluster membership at [clusterdestartups.org](/membresias).
