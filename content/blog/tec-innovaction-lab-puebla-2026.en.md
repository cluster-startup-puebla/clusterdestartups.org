On Friday, October 30, 2026, [Tecnológico de Monterrey, Campus Puebla](https://tec.mx/es/puebla), hosted the in-person final of [Tec InnovAction Lab](https://tecinnovactionlab.tecinnovactionlab.workers.dev/). The day ran from 8:30 to 18:00, with workshops, an elimination round, a Shark Tank, and the awards. **Álvaro Castillo Carreño**, vice president, attended for the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/).

Tec InnovAction Lab is a public-innovation laboratory. High school and university students design solutions for real challenges in their community. The School of Social Sciences and Government at the campus leads it, with **Maribel Flores Sánchez** as project lead and director of that school. The work uses human-centered design, critical thinking, data analysis, and collective intelligence, and it puts students in the same room as government, business, and civil society.

Proposals fall into three lines: an inclusive society and participatory governance, public transformation toward sustainability, and global and social challenges. Teams are four students from the same school, with an academic advisor.

The close was the winners announcement. On stage, the checks marked two categories, high school and university: 25,000 pesos for the winning teams and 12,500 for the finalists. The screen carried Tec InnovAction Lab, [Tekuno](https://www.tekuno.mx/), and the Cluster of Startups and Innovation.

For the Cluster, that final is the point where public innovation leaves the classroom and stands in front of allies. High school and university talent already brings a problem from its community, and the ecosystem can push it after the prize.

We thank Tec InnovAction Lab and Campus Puebla for the place at the awards.
