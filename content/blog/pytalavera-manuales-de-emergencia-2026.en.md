## Calm under the storm

On Thursday, August 6, 2026, the **PyTalavera** community held the meetup **'Calm under the storm: how to write emergency manuals'**, a technical incident-management workshop delivered by **Román Bernábe**, DevOps Engineer, at Workósfera Coworking (Cholula 35, La Paz, Puebla).

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation A.C. took part as a **collaborating organization**: it secured and set up the room to match capacity, equipped the session with furniture, a whiteboard and projection, coordinated the call and registration, and connected the local software development community with specialized technical training — free of charge for attendees, with no speaker fees and no registration cost.

## What was covered

Three and a half hours of technical content, referencing the FIRESCOPE, DevOps, Google SRE and ITIL frameworks:

- **The first 5 minutes** of an incident: an initial communication template — impact, suspected cause, commander, operator and messenger — with an update cadence of 10–20 minutes.
- **The irreversibility principle** in decision-making during an incident.
- **Roles, reporting cadence and escalation criteria**.
- **Blameless post mortem**: what happened, why and how to prevent it, analyzing the process and not the people.
- **Live exercise**: a database lockout case worked on the whiteboard, drafting the status update and assigning roles by name.

Participants walked away with runbook and incident communication templates ready to use in their operations.

## Tech community in the ecosystem

Nineteen people filled the room to 90% capacity. The meetup is part of the tech community line the Cluster supports with spaces and outreach: if your community wants a session, reach out through [membership](/membresias) or the contact form.
