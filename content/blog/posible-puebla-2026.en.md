## POSiBLE Puebla at the close of the camp

On Sunday, October 4, 2026, **Rodrigo Yamil Álvarez Moisés**, president of the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/), and **Álvaro Castillo Carreño**, vice president, took part as evaluators in the final pitch presentations of [POSiBLE](https://posible.org.mx/) National Camp 2026. The close ran from 10:00 to 14:00 at the Hotel City Express Plus by Marriott Santa Fe, in Mexico City.

[POSiBLE](https://posible.org.mx/) is Fundación Televisa's entrepreneurship program. The national camp is an intensive program of several days: mentorship, tools, and strengthening of projects. In this cohort, the close brought together 70 entrepreneurs from 17 states. From Puebla, the Cluster came to that table so POSiBLE Puebla would continue after the pitch.

We thank POSiBLE and Fundación Televisa for organizing the camp and for the trust to evaluate. The invitation came from **Alejandra Guerrero González**, POSiBLE's general coordinator.

## What the Cluster put on the table

The Cluster extended links with sectors that matter to the entrepreneurs. It also offered help to obtain the [Puebla Cinco de Mayo distinctive seal](https://cincodemayo.puebla.gob.mx/sello-distintivo.html), the state's mark for products, services, and ventures of Puebla origin, and ongoing mentorship so those projects can keep growing after the camp.

That is the POSiBLE Puebla path: someone who finishes the national program finds allies in the state's ecosystem, a route toward the seal, and support that continues after the Sunday of the pitch.

## More information

The program is at [POSiBLE](https://posible.org.mx/). The [Puebla Cinco de Mayo distinctive seal](https://cincodemayo.puebla.gob.mx/sello-distintivo.html) is granted by the state government. The association that attended as evaluator, and that opens those links from Puebla, is the Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.
