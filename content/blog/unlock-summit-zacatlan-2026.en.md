## The fourth edition in Zacatlán

**UNLOCK Summit 2026** took place from Friday, September 11 to Sunday, September 13 in Zacatlán de las Manzanas, Puebla. It was the fourth edition of the gathering and the second time the municipality has hosted it. The venue was the Centro Cultural de Zacatlán, in the heart of the town square.

The summit brings women entrepreneurs together for a three-day program of talks, panels, workshops, and space to connect. This edition's agenda crossed artificial intelligence, image, emotional intelligence, wellbeing, and purpose-driven business. Sunday the 13th closed with the Brunch de Consciencia. The Guerra de Pitches, in alliance with Ángulo, opened a space for women-led projects with impact.

[Karla Revilla](https://unlocksummit.io/unlock-summit-2026/), CEO of UNLOCK Summit, and the Municipality of Zacatlán, led by mayor Bety Sánchez, carried the gathering. Coverage of the opening is in [Parabólica](https://www.parabolica.mx/2026/estatal/item/11469-zacatlan-sede-de-la-cuarta-edicion-del-unlock-summit).

## The Cluster's participation

**Yamil Álvarez**, president of the Cluster of Startups and Innovation, was invited as a representative of the Ministry of Economic Development and Labor (**SEDETRA**) to the opening and to live the full UNLOCK Summit experience.

He spoke at the opening on Friday the 11th and stayed through all three days of the program, until the close on Sunday the 13th.

## Why it matters for the ecosystem

UNLOCK puts women's entrepreneurship, technology, and artificial intelligence in the same room, in one of Puebla's pueblos mágicos. For SEDETRA and for the Cluster, that mix is the point: innovation is also discussed where community, craft, and the will to build a company already exist, not only in the capital's software scene.

The Cluster president's presence, on behalf of SEDETRA, brings the startup ecosystem closer to a conversation that already mixes business, technology, and talent development. It is the same ground on which the Cluster works with government, chambers, and academia.
