## Collaboration agreement with AMMJE Angelópolis

On Thursday, September 17, 2026, at the first ordinary members' meeting of the Mexican Association of Women Business Owners (**AMMJE**), Angelópolis chapter, a **collaboration agreement** was signed with the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/). The session was led by the chapter president, **Edith Alamillo Montes**. The Cluster's president, **Yamil Álvarez**, signed for the association.

The collaboration agreement is the instrument. Its public purpose is to connect member business owners with opportunities in Puebla's innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem: the [startups](/en/empresas/), programs, and spaces the CSI articulates across government, chambers, academia, and companies.

## The 2026–2028 roadmap

At the same meeting, the thirteen members of AMMJE Angelópolis's board presented the first-year plan of the 2026–2028 term: training, commercial linkage, digital expansion, community impact, and international projection. The chapter holds a seat on the Business Advisory Council of the Puebla State Congress, from which it seeks to bring women business owners into the competitiveness agenda.

That day a second collaboration agreement was also signed, with the outlet **Liderin**, led by Vicky Fuentes, to open coverage for members' companies and careers.

In [Perspectivas MX](https://perspectivas-mx.com/ammje-angelopolis-firma-dos-convenios-y-presenta-su-plan-de-trabajo-2026-2028-en-su-primera-reunion-ordinaria/) coverage, Alamillo Montes said in her welcome: "None of us has to walk alone. Together we go further." She added that the term will be measured and documented with figures.

## Why it matters for the ecosystem

A collaboration agreement requires both sides to move from the photograph to the work. For the CSI, that means opening the ecosystem it already runs —companies, talent, and programs— to women who lead firms and are looking for technology, suppliers, and scale. For AMMJE Angelópolis, it is a concrete piece of the road to 2028: linkage that can be counted, not only announced.

Days earlier, the same Cluster president was at [UNLOCK Summit 2026](/en/blog/unlock-summit-zacatlan-2026/) as SEDETRA's representative, at a gathering of women entrepreneurs in Zacatlán. The agreement with AMMJE Angelópolis continues that line: innovation in Puebla is also built with people who already run a company.
