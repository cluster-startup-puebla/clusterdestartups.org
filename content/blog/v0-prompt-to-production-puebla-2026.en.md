## From idea to product in minutes

On Saturday, February 7, 2026, Puebla joined the **v0 Global Buildathon** — the simultaneous AI building event held in more than 40 cities worldwide — with the local edition **Prompt to Production, Puebla City**.

The session was hosted by **Álvaro Castillo**, Cluster Manager of the Cluster of Startups and Innovation, with an operations team from **Sabia**, in an event that brought the global v0 by Vercel buildathon link to the city for the first time.

## How the format worked

A fast-forward buildathon: going from idea to product in minutes, creating professional web apps and sites with AI. The call included:

- **v0 credits included** for every participant, redeemable during the session.
- **Live building** with no prior technical knowledge required: bring an idea and the willingness to build it.
- **Networking with the local tech community**, with support from VK, Líder 360°, Go Go Track and Talavera Valley.

The profile was deliberately inclusive: aimed at people with no prior programming experience — founders with an idea, operators with a process to digitalize, and the AI-curious.

## The start of a line

This event opened the AI community line that later continued with the Cafe Cursor in May and Prompt to Product in August, always with the same mechanism: tool credits, mandatory laptop and live building.

Upcoming sessions of the community line are announced first to [Cluster](/membresias) membership.
