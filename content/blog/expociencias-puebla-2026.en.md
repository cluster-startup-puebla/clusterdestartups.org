## ExpoCiencias Puebla on the way to the national fair

On Monday, October 5, 2026, [UPAEP](https://upaep.mx/) hosted a business breakfast to introduce the Puebla delegation that will represent the state at [ExpoCiencias Nacional 2026](https://expocienciasnacional.laredmex.org/). The national meeting runs November 11–14 at CINTERMEX in Monterrey.

The delegation brings together 62 students and 28 advisors, with 28 research and innovation projects. Those projects aim to address problems in technology, the environment, and society.

**José Daniel Martínez Cuspinera**, secretary of the board, attended for the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/).

## What the secretary shared

At the breakfast, José Daniel Martínez Cuspinera said: "Whatever the education level, projects have to be of quality, and as allies we have to push so we can give those projects momentum."

That is the ExpoCiencias Puebla path: the quality of the work matters in preschool, in upper secondary school, and at university, and the state's ecosystem can push so this delegation arrives in Monterrey with strength.

We thank UPAEP for organizing the breakfast and for the chance to meet the students who will do science with impact from Puebla.

## More information

The national venue is on [ExpoCiencias Nacional 2026](https://expocienciasnacional.laredmex.org/). The university that called the breakfast is [UPAEP](https://upaep.mx/). The person who attended for the association is the secretary of the Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.
