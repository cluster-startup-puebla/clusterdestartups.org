## Puebla at the national inauguration

On Monday, October 5, 2026, **Rodrigo Yamil Álvarez Moisés**, president of the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/), took part in the national inauguration of World Space Week Mexico 2026 at the Chamber of Deputies in Mexico City. He spoke on behalf of Puebla's innovation ecosystem.

The gathering was convened by [Regenia](https://regeniamexico.org/), the Network for the Generation of Knowledge and Open Innovation, and by the National Coordination of World Space Week Mexico. This year's theme is **Rocket Revolution**. Deputies María Teresa Ealy Díaz and Herminia López Santiago inaugurated the event. The broadcast is on the [Canal del Congreso México](https://www.youtube.com/live/svMB9LN9-rQ). Yamil Álvarez's remarks are at [this point in the broadcast](https://www.youtube.com/live/svMB9LN9-rQ?t=9476).

## What Puebla's ecosystem put on the table

The message was concrete: there is room to build companies in the aerospace industry, and local startups grow when government, academia, and business pull in the same direction.

That reading was already in motion. On September 25 the CSI visited the [Aeronautical University in Querétaro](/en/blog/visita-unaq-space-tech-2026/) to open collaboration on Space Tech startups. The October 5 inauguration carries that idea into the week when the country talks about space. Puebla arrives with a cluster that connects the people who can turn aerospace talent into a company.

## What World Space Week is

[World Space Week](https://www.un.org/en/observances/world-space-week) is held every year from **October 4 to 10**. The United Nations General Assembly declared it on December 6, 1999, in resolution 54/68, to recognize what space science and technology contribute to people's lives. It is the world's largest space-outreach event. International coordination sits with the UN Office for Outer Space Affairs and the World Space Week Association.

The dates mark two milestones. On October 4, 1957, Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite, was launched. On October 10, 1967, the Outer Space Treaty entered into force, the first agreement among nations on the peaceful use of outer space.

In Mexico the week is organized as [World Space Week Mexico](https://sme-mexico.space/2026/). National participation began in 2009 with Universum, the UNAM science museum. In 2013 the Mexican Space Agency pushed the program across the country. Between 2014 and 2018 there were activities in all 32 states, and Mexico reached third place worldwide in number of venues. In 2024 the program passed 700 activities.

The 2026 theme, **Rocket Revolution**, is about who can reach space. Startups, universities, and student teams design, build, and launch space technology. In Mexico, that is the door through which aerospace entrepreneurship becomes an industry.

## Regenia

[Regenia](https://regeniamexico.org/) is the Network for the Generation of Knowledge and Open Innovation: a national, independent, nonpartisan network that brings together science communicators, universities, institutions, companies, governments, and citizens so scientific and technological knowledge can circulate and be used.

At the inauguration, Regenia convened the gathering together with the National Coordination of World Space Week Mexico. Its president, Denzel Carrillo, described it there as a bridge among industry, government, academia, and civil society. For a startup cluster, that bridge is the work: an aerospace startup scales when outreach, the university, and public policy sit at the same table.

## The pact for space outreach

The Cluster announced that it will sign, in collaboration with [UPAEP](https://upaep.mx/licenciaturas/ingenieria-aeroespacial), the **pact for space outreach**.

UPAEP trains aerospace engineers in Puebla. The pact places that outreach work next to the startup ecosystem: bringing the aerospace industry closer to more people in the state and opening a path for anyone who wants to build a company in it. The October 5 participation puts that announcement on the record at the national inauguration.
