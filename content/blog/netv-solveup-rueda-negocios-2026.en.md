## The night at Workósfera

**NETV** —NETV Business Global Community— is a business community born in Puebla and a cluster startup. The next customer, supplier or partner is already in the market, but the contact lives on business cards, WhatsApp groups and coffees that never happen. A congress lasts a few days, and what was discussed there is lost when it ends. NETV’s value is bringing that into one ecosystem: artificial intelligence finds who is useful to whom, and events plus commercial tools turn that connection into a contract. The Digital Venue keeps the lobby, auditoriums, expo and deal room alongside the in-person event, and what happens there stays on record so selling can continue afterward.

**Francisco Vara** (Francisco José Vara Trujillo), CEO, leads it. His work is business linkage: networking, expos, business tables and interviews, online, hybrid and in person.

On Thursday, October 1, 2026, he brought that work into the room. **NETV** held **Networking y rueda de negocios** in the **NETV & StartUp SolveUp** format at Workósfera Coworking (Cholula 35, La Paz, Puebla). The meeting was hybrid and free, with check-in from 5:30 p.m. and a program from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. The Startup and Innovation Cluster joined the event because the format takes on, in practice, the disconnect between industry and startups in Puebla.

## NETV & StartUp SolveUp, a room for saying the problem out loud

Every company carries at least one problem its own team has not been able to solve. It comes up at business lunches, in a board meeting, or with the friend who runs another company. It is almost never said out loud in front of people whose work is to solve that kind of thing.

**NETV & StartUp SolveUp** is that room. Companies present, one minute each, two things: what they sell and what hurts. No corporate deck and no rehearsed speech. On the other side, startups do not come to sell. They come to listen. Those who understand the problems best get, three weeks later, a chance to present their solution at **Demo & Validation Day**, in front of the same business owners who named the problems.

## How the night was built

- **5:30–6:00 p.m.** Check-in and accreditation.
- **6:00–6:09 p.m.** Welcome and why this format.
- **6:09–6:25 p.m.** The companies introduce themselves.
- **6:25–6:45 p.m.** Six challenges on the table.
- **6:45–6:50 p.m.** Tables are formed.
- **6:50–7:30 p.m.** Solution tables and business rounds.
- **7:30–7:48 p.m.** Commitments said out loud, and next steps.
- **7:48–8:00 p.m.** Close —what was agreed lands on WhatsApp— and the group photo.

## Why it matters to the cluster

In Puebla, the plant and the startup rarely meet. Industry has the problem; the startup has a tool and sometimes arrives ready to sell it before listening. This round reverses the order: the pain first, in one minute and without a pitch, and the table after that.

The cluster joined for two reasons. The event belongs to a startup in the directory. And the format answers the gap the cluster works on every day: Puebla industry and startups meeting with a real problem on the table.
