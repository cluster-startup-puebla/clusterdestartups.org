## Space and company

On Thursday, May 14, 2026, **Workósfera Coworking** (Cholula 35, La Paz, Puebla) hosted **51 people** at the **Cafe Cursor Puebla**, a Cafe Takeover format organized by Kevin Jiménez, Vahagn Chavushyan, Álvaro Castillo — Cluster Manager of the Cluster of Startups and Innovation — and the Sabia team.

The proposal was deliberately open: no talks, no rigid agenda. A meeting point to bring your laptop, drink coffee and push your own projects alongside others exploring AI, with official ID and personal QR code at the door.

## Four things to do — none mandatory

- **Code and move forward**: pick up a paused project and push it with Cursor.
- **Connect**: open to founders, CEOs and non-technical profiles, not just developers.
- **Real feedback**: show what you're building, solve technical doubts and see how others work up close.
- **Community**: meet the people moving technology in Puebla in a relaxed setting.

Coffee and tea were on the house, and **Cursor credits** were given away to use Pro features during the event, redeemable on the spot with each participant's laptop.

## What the attendance says

It was the best-documented attendance of the year for the community line in Puebla — above technical meetups with talks and workshops with full agendas. The call didn't promise content: it promised space and company. For this audience, that is exactly what drives the conversation.

The format will repeat. Dates are announced first to [Cluster](/membresias) membership and on the community's channels.
