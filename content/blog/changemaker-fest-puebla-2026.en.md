## ChangeMaker Fest Puebla, with the CSI treasurer

On Thursday, October 8, 2026, [Tecnológico de Monterrey, Puebla campus](https://tec.mx/es/puebla) and [Grupo Proactivo Mexicano](https://www.grupoproactivo.org/) held ChangeMaker Fest PUE 2026 at the campus South Library. The day brought together a panel, talks, and workshops from the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

**Rodrigo Ruiz**, treasurer of the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/) and CEO of [Kotemah](/en/empresas/kotemah/), was invited to the gathering. In the South Library Auditorium, inside the ImpactoN20 block, he gave the talk “Exoskeletons: reality or fiction?”. The auditorium screen carried his name and that title. Kotemah designs exoskeletons and load-assist equipment for industry.

## How the day was set

The program at the South Library ran like this:

- 9:00 to 9:59, registration at the entrance of the South Library Auditorium.
- 10:00 to 10:30, opening and welcome.
- 10:30 to 11:30, panel.
- 11:30 to 12:00, coffee break.
- 12:00 to 1:15 p.m., ImpactoN20: three 20-minute talks.
- 1:15 to 2:45 p.m., workshops in the South Library green area.

In that same ImpactoN20 block, [El Heraldo de Puebla](https://heraldodepuebla.com/2026/10/08/grupo-proactivo-mexicano-y-tec-de-monterrey-realizan-change-maker-fest-puebla-2026/) also recorded Eduardo Martínez, with “In Mexico, is language a barrier to living?”, and Cecilia Rendón, with “Technology and tradition.” The workshops were “Organic alchemy,” by Jorge Alberto Cueto, and “Mindset of the change agent,” by Giselle Kuro.

Carlos Prado Guerrero, the campus director of Leadership and Training, framed the fest as a place for young people and entrepreneurs to leave with knowledge, connections, and another way of doing things. Patricio Zorrilla, president and founding partner of Grupo Proactivo Mexicano, stressed that every venture has to leave a positive impact on society.

We thank Tecnológico de Monterrey, Puebla campus, and Grupo Proactivo Mexicano for the invitation that put Kotemah and the Cluster at that table.
