## Know, Connect, Collaborate

On September 10, 2026, **Sinergia Clústeres Puebla** was held at the William O. Jenkins Convention Center Auditorium, an event convened by the Secretariat of Economic Development and Labor of the State Government of Puebla, in coordination with the business sector and the state's 24 productive clusters.

Under the motto **Know, Connect, Collaborate**, the gathering aimed to build concrete agreements to strengthen value chains and generate new opportunities for growth and development for Puebla.

## The Cluster of Startups and Innovation's participation

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation was present through **Yamil Álvarez**, vice president of the Inter-Cluster Council, who actively participated in the event's working sessions and networking. During the activity, **10 clusters** presented in lightning talk format what they do, how they can collaborate with other clusters, and how to generate **synergy** among the state's various productive sectors.

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation's presentation emphasized the role that technology, talent and startups play in modernizing Puebla's established industry, as well as the Innovation Hub infrastructure as a meeting point for the ecosystem.

## An integrated ecosystem

The event featured participation from representatives of Puebla's 24 productive clusters, state authorities and business sector representatives. The opening lecture addressed **"The value of an integrated ecosystem in the state's economic system"**, setting the stage for the day's discussions and agreements.

The program included two cluster presentation blocks (10 per cluster, with 5 minutes each), networking sessions and an Inter-Cluster Council meeting to define next steps.

## Moving forward

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation continues with the vocation of being transversal: uniting startups, industry, academia and government to build a stronger technological ecosystem in Puebla. Our goal is clear — modernize the industry, generate economic development, welfare and competitiveness for the state.

We call for collaboration and unity among all actors in the ecosystem. The Cluster's door is always open for those who want to join this goal.

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation thanks **SEDETRA** for creating these meeting spaces and the **Inter-Cluster Council** for its leadership in building agreements that benefit all of Puebla.
