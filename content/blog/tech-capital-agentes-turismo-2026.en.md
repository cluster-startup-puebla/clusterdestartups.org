On Saturday, October 17, 2026, at the [Puebla Exhibition Center](https://techcapitalpuebla.com/), the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/) brought to Tech Capital the workshop “Your team of AI agents for the tourism sector.” **Kevin Jiménez**, a SpaceXAI ambassador, led it.

[Tech Capital 2026](https://techcapitalpuebla.com/) is the state’s digital-tourism meeting. The Secretariat of Science, Humanities, Technology and Innovation and the Secretariat of Tourism Development organize it, from October 15 to 17, with free admission. This edition puts digital tourism at the center, ahead of Tianguis Turístico México 2027. The [program](https://techcapitalpuebla.com/program) placed the Cluster in the workshop room at 11:00 on the third day.

The session did not stay a talk about the future of travel. The title says what it was: building a team of agents for the tourism sector. On the screen was [Grok Bot](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot), framed as AI teammates that have their own computer and finish the work. It is not a chat that returns a draft. It is an agent that goes into the tools, stays on the task, and comes back when a decision is needed.

That is what the workshop brought down to the tourism floor. A hotel, an agency, or a destination already lives in bookings, messages, content, and operations. Saturday’s question was which part of that work can sit with a team of agents, rather than with a one-off prompt.

Kevin Jiménez is the same host of the Cluster’s AI community line. He was at the [May Cafe Cursor](/en/blog/cafe-cursor-puebla-2026/) and at [Prompt to Product](/en/blog/prompt-to-product-cursor-workshop-2026/) in August. Since August 14, the Cursor ambassador program has been called SpaceXAI. This workshop carries that community into the state’s tourism meeting.

We thank Kevin Jiménez for the workshop and Tech Capital for the room.
