## A dialogue between Puebla and Querétaro

On Thursday, September 24, 2026, the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](/en/quienes-somos/) met with **Rodrigo Ruiz Ballesteros**, secretary of Innovation and Technology of the municipality of Querétaro. The Cluster was represented by its president, **Yamil Álvarez**, and cluster manager **Álvaro Castillo**.

The conversation was about the role of innovation and technology in opening opportunities and strengthening ecosystems. Querétaro told it toward its own state. From Puebla, the same meeting opens another door: dialogue and collaboration to build a technology ecosystem that crosses the line between the two states.

## What each side already has

Querétaro has spent years building digital infrastructure, talent, and a public innovation center. Ruiz Ballesteros coordinates **BLOQUE**, the municipality's innovation and technology center, with training in artificial intelligence, data, and entrepreneurship, alongside academia and companies. That account is in the [Alternativo MX](https://alternativo.mx/rodrigo-ruiz-bloque-innovacion-tecnologia-queretaro/) interview.

Puebla comes to the table with a cluster that already articulates government, chambers, academia, and [startups](/en/empresas/). A month earlier, the CSI operated the [Puebla Pavilion at InnovaFest Querétaro 2026](/en/blog/pabellon-puebla-innovafest-queretaro-2026/). This meeting continues that line: stop merely coinciding at an event and sit down to talk about an ecosystem.

## An ecosystem that crosses both states

A technology ecosystem is not decreed inside a single municipality. It takes shape when industry, academia, and productive sectors can move talent, projects, and supply from one state to the other.

For Puebla, the dialogue is useful if that proximity becomes work: Puebla startups that find industry and talent in Querétaro, and Querétaro projects that find in Puebla the capacity to innovate. The September 24 meeting opened that collaboration.

## The post

We thank Rodrigo Ruiz Ballesteros for recording the meeting on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdsCbzYtWWN/). That note looks toward Querétaro. The association at the table is the Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C., and from here the emphasis is Puebla: keep the dialogue so the two technology ecosystems are built together.
