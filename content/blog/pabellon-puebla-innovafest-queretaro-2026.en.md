## The Puebla Pavilion

The Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C. from Puebla, led by its president **Yamil Álvarez**, organized and operated the **Puebla Pavilion** at InnovaFest Querétaro 2026, held on Friday, August 21, 2026 at the Querétaro Centro de Congresos (QCC).

Under the institutional identity of the Government of the State of Puebla, the pavilion brought together **nine actors from the Puebla innovation ecosystem** across eight exhibit modules during seven continuous hours of operation, funded by the Government of the State of Puebla itself: the Cluster acted as coordinator and operator of the space without committing its own resources.

## A pavilion for the whole Puebla ecosystem

The Puebla Pavilion brought together the four vertices of the Cluster's articulation model — government, chambers, academia and the private sector — with the State also taking part as a business operator:

- **Clúster de Plásticos y Economía Circular** — Circular Economy Agenda 2030
- **Química Maya** — sustainable housing project
- **Clúster de Startups e Innovación A.C.** — pavilion coordination and ecosystem agenda
- **Kotemah** — exoskeletons and robotics for assisted lifting
- **Oxtron** — greenhouse gas capture and reuse
- **Universidad Iberoamericana Puebla (IDIT)** — university technology development
- **COPARMEX Puebla** — Startup Forum
- **Yankuilotl** — software factory, board manufacturing and personal electric mobility
- **Traixo** — parcel logistics with an innovative operating model

The eight modules covered **six technology axes**: circular economy and sustainability, advanced manufacturing and robotics, mobility, logistics, health and medical technology, and ecosystem development.

## How the space was run

The pavilion operated on a replicable design for future state participations. On 25 m² of contracted floor area (five by five meters), eight 1.20 m-wide exhibit modules ran under a **two-zone attention model**: short conversations at each module, three to five minutes, and substantive conversations in an adjacent networking area with three shared tables, fifteen to twenty-five minutes per counterpart.

This separation of long conversations allowed all eight modules to operate throughout the day without blocking one another, each attending visitors continuously.

## Result: InnovaFest Puebla Organizing Committee

InnovaFest's National Coordination, headed by Dr. Cecilia Bañuelos Barrón, Head of the Innovation Sector at Mexico's Ministry of Economy, visited the Puebla Pavilion and held a working meeting with the Cluster's delegation, led by president Yamil Álvarez. As a follow-up action, the **establishment of the InnovaFest Puebla Organizing Committee** was agreed, the first step toward Puebla's candidacy to host the 2027 edition.

The event's opening presidium mentioned Puebla — alongside Querétaro, Hidalgo, Tlaxcala, Estado de México, Ciudad de México, Yucatán, Guanajuato and San Luis Potosí — among the states with talent represented at the gathering, with press coverage from August 21 and photo records as evidence.

## About InnovaFest

InnovaFest is Mexico's national innovation gathering, driven by the Ministry of Economy and the host state governments. The Querétaro 2026 edition drew around 5,000 expected attendees, more than 700 exhibitors, 400 exhibited prototypes and 75 investment funds, with over 3,100 applications to the Premio a la Innovación Mexicana and free admission to the public.
